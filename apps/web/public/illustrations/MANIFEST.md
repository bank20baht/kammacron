# Rural workshop object illustrations

Current direction: humble, durable, familiar things from a Southern Thai rural working household
or workshop. One main object, plain practical construction, honest material texture and modest
wear. No kanok/flame motifs, lotus emblems, filigree, carved borders or ceremonial/luxury styling.
The buffalo remains the primary brand logo, separate from small card decorations.

All ten assets are **384 × 384 WebP, 1:1**, alpha-transparent with uncropped silhouettes, intended
for 48–96px display (up to 128px for empty clouds). Fine cobalt/white/light-blue printmaking grain
is retained; restrained orange is used for a functional part, rust or worn paint. The banknote
accent uses faded gray-green within artwork only. No new functional UI color tokens are added.

The earlier ornate objects are archived in `docs/design/ornamental-object-archive/`; landscapes
remain in `docs/design/landscape-archive/`. Neither archive is shipped as runtime decoration.
Current URLs use `v=workshop-2` to refresh caches. Both themes use the same transparent objects.
Labels, metrics, statuses and actual dates remain real UI content.

## Quiet Bank signature

A small folded banknote corner is tucked behind the calendar's upper-right binding, and a tiny
edge peeks from the toolbox lid seam. Each remains below 5% of its main object's area and is easy
to miss at UI size. The cost card uses one folded note in its existing small decorative corner.
These are generic illustrated notes with abstract faint hatching, no actual denomination, portrait,
official seal, serial number or readable text. No currency data is baked into the art.

## Generation

Built-in imagegen edits each previous object (hourglass → mug, loupe → standing mirror,
coin → folded banknote). The resulting 1254 × 1254 PNGs are resized and encoded as transparent
WebP, quality 85. No background, CSS mask, theme filter, blend or new animation is applied.

Shared edit direction followed by the subject-specific requirements:

> Edit target: the attached current kammacron decorative object. Replace its ornamental treatment with an ORDINARY, WELL-USED, DURABLE everyday item from a working household, rubber smallholding or rural workshop in SOUTHERN THAILAND. The brand means manual laborer: humble, practical, sturdy, tangible, economical, cared for and used for years. Preserve the airy hand-drawn cobalt/white/light-blue Risograph linework and light stippled print grain, with very restrained orange accents, but give objects honest material texture, blunt simple construction, modest scuffs, dents, paint chips and plain joints. Absolutely NO KANOK, Thai flame motifs, lotus emblems, floral engraving, paisley, arabesques, temple decoration, decorative finials, carved borders, polished luxury, ceremonial objects, palace ornament or fantasy. Do not decorate plain surfaces. Tiny orange areas may be rust, paint wear or a practical part, not ornamental jewelry. One isolated main object, square 1:1 canvas, centered with 12% transparent safety margins, complete silhouette, genuinely TRANSPARENT background. Readable at 48–96px. No person, animal, buffalo, scenery, buildings, tabletop scene, floor, landscape, cast shadow, glowing halo, backdrop, white rectangle or frame surrounding the asset. No text, letters, logo, denomination, numeral, watermark, photorealism or glossy 3D. No added money except the specific subtle banknote requested in that subject.
